# Your offers, from your phone

Each offer is a page with your photos, your packages and your prices. You make it in ChatGPT, the
way you would ask an assistant.

## Always start in "Offers"

Open ChatGPT and tap "Offers" under Projects. Write there. Only there does it know your packages
and where the offer goes. In any other chat it won't work.

## New offer

Say in your own words who they are, what they are celebrating, when, where, and which package you
suggest. For example:

> New offer for Maria and Nikos, wedding 14/6/2027 in Agia Pelagia, I suggest Gold with drone.

If something is missing, it will ask. In a minute or two it replies with two links:

- the **preview**, so you see the page before the client does,
- the **client link**, which works once you tap Merge.

A different price for this couple? Say so: "Gold at 1,300".
Want a few words to the client on the page? Write them too, they go in as you wrote them.

## Inquiries from email

Every hour ChatGPT looks at new inquiries in your Gmail. For each one it opens a draft offer and
leaves a reply ready in your Drafts, in the same conversation. It sends nothing. Don't want to wait?
Write in "Offers":

> Check my inquiries.

Before you send the reply, look at the offer. If the client didn't ask for a package, the page
highlights the middle one. Want another? Say so in "Offers". Then tap Merge, put the client link
where it says [LINK], and check who the reply goes to before you send it.

Now and then, look at the **Offers/needs-you** label in Gmail. Mails that need you wait there: it
wasn't sure the mail was an inquiry, it found no client address, or something went wrong. If a day
goes by with no notification from ChatGPT, something is stuck. Write "Check my inquiries" in
"Offers" and it will tell you what is wrong.

## Photos from your phone

Your best photos are already on the page. If you want some just for this client, pick them in the
Photos app and send them in the chat: "add these to Maria's offer".

Screenshots don't go in. If it asks for a photo again, send it from Photos.

## Booked or lost

- "Maria and Nikos booked Gold with drone." If you don't say the package, it will ask.
- "Giannis's offer is lost." The page stays as it is, it is just marked as not going ahead.

## Merge only on green

Every change goes through a check on GitHub before it goes live.

- **Green tick:** all good. Tap **Merge** and the page is ready in about two minutes.
- **Red cross:** don't tap Merge. Tell ChatGPT "the check failed, fix it". It fixes it and sends
  you the links again.

As long as you don't tap Merge, the client sees nothing and nothing breaks.

## Send the link

After Merge, copy the client link and send it the way you usually talk to them: Viber, WhatsApp or
email. From the page they can answer you in one tap.
